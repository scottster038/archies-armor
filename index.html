The Family

The Diagnosis & The Search for Answers

From early infancy, Archie showed subtle developmental delays and unexpected neurological symptoms. After months of medical testing, consultations with specialists, and whole genome sequencing, we received the diagnosis: Archie has a rare, de novo mutation in the CACNA1A gene.

The CACNA1A gene provides instructions for making a key subunit of the CaV2.1 calcium channel, vital for normal communication between brain cells. Archie’s specific variant disrupts this delicate signaling pathway, leading to challenges with motor function, speech, and potential seizure activity.

Rare DNA Variant

Archie’s single nucleotide change requires targeted personalized medicine rather than standard off-the-shelf pharmaceuticals.

ASO Breakthrough

Working with the n-Lorem Foundation to design a custom antisense oligonucleotide (ASO) to silence or correct the faulty gene copy.

Medical Science

Understanding CACNA1A

What is the CACNA1A gene, how does it function, and why are targeted genetic therapies so transformational?

Calcium Channels

The CACNA1A gene encodes the alpha-1A subunit of the CaV2.1 P/Q-type voltage-gated calcium channel, controlling neurotransmitter release in brain neurons.

Crucial for brain communication →

Variants & Function

Mutations can be “gain-of-function” (an overactive channel) or “loss-of-function” (an underactive channel). Identifying Archie’s exact variant is key to tailoring treatment.

Precision variant mapping →

Targeted ASO Therapy

Antisense oligonucleotides (ASOs) are short synthetic strands of nucleic acid designed to bind specifically to mutant mRNA to normalize protein expression.

n-Lorem nano-rare program →

Comparing Traditional vs. Personalized Genetic Medicine

Standard Symptom Management

  • Treats symptoms (seizures, ataxia) without addressing the underlying DNA cause.
  • Trial-and-error medication cycles with significant side effects.
  • Not specific to individual CACNA1A single-letter mutations.

Custom ASO Therapy (Archie’s Plan)

  • Specifically targets Archie’s exact genetic mutation sequence.
  • Designed to restore proper calcium channel function in neurons.
  • Discovery and development provided free by n-Lorem; the family funds hospital delivery and administration.

The Roadmap to Healing

Treatment, Research & ASO Progress

Developing a personalized medicine requires meticulous research, laboratory validation, and hospital-based intrathecal administration.

  1. Completed

    1. Genetic Diagnosis & n-Lorem Acceptance

    In Progress

    2. ASO Design & iPSC Cell Testing

    Next Step
