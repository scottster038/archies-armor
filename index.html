Understanding the diagnosis

What is CACNA1A?

CACNA1A is a gene that gives the body instructions for building part of a calcium channel found on nerve cells in the brain. These channels help neurons send signals to one another. When the gene has a change (a “variant”), those signals can be disrupted.

CACNA1A-related disorders are rare and look different in every child. They can affect:

  • Balance & coordination (ataxia)
  • Muscle tone
  • Eye movements
  • Speech & development
  • Seizures
  • Hemiplegic migraine
